# Design System

## Brand
**Brand:** AR Events Co.

**Tagline:** Your Celebration, Our Passion

## Visual Direction
Premium, elegant, celebratory, trustworthy, and locally appropriate for Islamabad/Rawalpindi.

## Primary Theme
- Deep navy / midnight background
- Metallic gold accents
- White / warm-neutral text surfaces
- Optional navy-blue secondary accents

## Typography
Use a refined serif/display typeface for brand and major headings, paired with a clean modern sans-serif for UI/body copy.

## Logo Usage
- Primary logo for dark hero/brand contexts.
- Simplified logo/icon for favicon and compact spaces.
- Do not distort, recolor, or add effects outside the approved brand system.

## UI Principles
- 8px spacing rhythm where practical.
- Clear card hierarchy.
- Rounded corners kept moderate and consistent.
- Subtle shadows, not heavy glass effects everywhere.
- High contrast for text and controls.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG AA for text and interactive controls. Keyboard navigation and visible focus states are mandatory.

## Responsive Breakpoints
Design mobile-first, then tablet, laptop, and wide desktop layouts. Do not tie layout logic to one device model.
