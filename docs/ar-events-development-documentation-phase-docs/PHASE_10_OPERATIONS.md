# Phase 10 — Payments, Notifications & Documents

## Objective

Complete the operational workflow around money, customer communication, and business paperwork.

## Payments

Support the selected payment methods through a provider abstraction. At launch, bank transfer/manual confirmation may be supported alongside online payment where commercially justified.

Track:

- Amount due
- Deposit
- Amount paid
- Remaining balance
- Payment method
- Transaction/reference ID
- Refunds
- Payment timestamps

## Notifications

Customer events:

- Booking received
- Booking confirmed
- Payment recorded
- Payment reminder
- Event reminder
- Event completed
- Review request

Admin events:

- New booking
- Payment received
- Cancellation
- Customer message

## Documents

- Booking confirmation
- Invoice
- Receipt where applicable
- Event summary

## Exit Criteria

Critical booking events generate the intended records and notifications, and payment data is reconciled with booking data.
